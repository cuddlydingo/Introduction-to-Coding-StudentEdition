# Grading and Academic Integrity Framework

## Suggested grading weights

- 40% Weekly homework (individual).
- 25% Collaborative game project.
- 20% Final individual coding project.
- 10% Research paper.
- 5% Participation/professional habits (preparedness, code walkthroughs, peer collaboration).

## Homework rubric (10 points each)

- 4 points: correctness (runs and meets requirements).
- 2 points: readability (naming, formatting, structure).
- 2 points: evidence of testing/debugging.
- 2 points: on-time submission and required file naming.

### Written-deliverable rubric (10 points each)

Several weeks submit `.txt`/`.md` artifacts (reflections, bug logs, pseudocode,
analysis, function maps) that do not "run." Grade those with this parallel
rubric:

- 4 points: completeness (addresses every part of the prompt).
- 3 points: clarity and organization (easy to follow; correct terminology).
- 2 points: evidence of thinking (specific examples, not vague generalities).
- 1 point: on-time submission and required file naming.

## AI-use policy implementation

Because AI-generated code/content is not allowed:

1. Students sign an [honor statement](../syllabus/academic-honor-statement.md) in Week 1.
2. Students maintain visible commit history for all major assignments.
3. Instructor performs short code walkthrough spot checks.
4. Any submission a student cannot explain may be reassessed in-class.
5. Repeated violations follow school academic integrity policy.

## Helpful practice policies

- Allow one homework revision per term after feedback.
- Drop the lowest weekly homework grade to support learning recovery.
- Encourage early submissions and iterative commits rather than single last-minute pushes.

### Suggested late-work window (adjust to your school policy)

- Full credit: merged before the next class.
- Up to 3 days late: minus 10% per day.
- After 3 days: accepted for up to 50% until the end of the unit, so students
  still complete the learning.
- Resubmissions: one revision per assignment within one week of feedback,
  regrading up to full credit.
